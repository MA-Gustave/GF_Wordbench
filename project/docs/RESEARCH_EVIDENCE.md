## Rules

- Every project-specific linguistic claim cites a source, elicitation record, corpus observation or reproducible GF experiment.
- General GF or RGL documentation does not by itself prove how Albanian is implemented here.
- Historical diagnostics do not establish current source status.
- Conflicting sources remain visible until resolved by a project decision.
- Research evidence never overrides current GF compile and scenario evidence for implementation status.
